# Portfolio replay

**Correction (2026-10-12).** An earlier version of this file (commit d7f2a7e) reported worst falls of 25-35%.
That was wrong, for two reasons: (1) cash from selling half a position was only credited when the whole position
closed, so equity was understated while a trade was open; (2) the local .env sets the drawdown halt to 20%, not the
settled 15%, and the simulator read it. Both are fixed; the simulator now pins 15% and 1% risk per trade itself.
With the fix the model's worst fall was 11-14% and the 15% trigger never fired, so the three "at a 15% fall"
versions are identical for the model. They differ only in the random-pick rows, where selling did not help.

Limits:
- Whole shares; cap tiers, liquidity ceiling and the regime ceiling are not applied.
- No market-mode history exists for these days, so every day is NORMAL (top 5 a day, full size). This is NOT the brain's real behaviour and the effect on the results is unknown: DEFENSIVE days would have bought fewer and smaller positions, which changes both losses and gains. Do not read this replay as conservative.
- The drawdown control checks closing equity and sells at the next open, so a gap can carry the loss past 15%. It is a trigger, not a guaranteed ceiling.
- 'Reduce' and 'sell all' wait 10 trading days after firing, then take the account's value that day as the new peak; the worst fall shown always uses the true running peak.
- Universe is today's watchlist (survivor bias). Daily candles: a bar touching both stop and target counts as the stop.
- Sizing is 1% risk at a 4% stop, capped by the capital ladder (about 25% of the account at most).
