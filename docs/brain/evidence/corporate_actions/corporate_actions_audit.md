## (i) Summary of findings

1. **Splits and bonuses are already adjusted in our candles (VERIFIED by data + VERIFIED external dates).** Five known split/bonus ex-dates inside the 49 show no price step and no volume step: NESTLEIND 1:10 split (ex 2024-01-05: close -1.7%, open gap +1.6%), BAJFINANCE 1:2 split + 4:1 bonus (ex 2025-06-16: +0.5%), DRREDDY 1:5 split (ex 2024-10-28: +0.7%), RELIANCE 1:1 bonus (ex 2024-10-28: +0.5%), TATASTEEL 1:10 split (ex 2022-07-28: +4.8%, a real rally per Business Standard). Volume is also adjusted (e.g. NESTLEIND volume ~2.6M/day in Jan 2024, about 10x the real pre-split share count). The ratio-signature scan found **zero** unadjusted split/bonus steps in the 306.
2. **Demergers / spin-offs are NOT cleanly adjusted. They leave a mechanical step in the series.** 14 events found (10 VERIFIED, 4 PARTIALLY by external record for the event itself, see table A). Size of the residual step in our data: ABFRL -55.9%, TRIVENI -41.6%, ALLCARGO -60.1% and -21.0%, SANOFI -28.0%, VEDL -32.9%, SIEMENS -25.1%, TMPV -13.1%, NMDC +85.7%, BAJAJELEC +38.8%, SCI +30.0%, ITC +6.3%, HINDUNILVR -5.0%, RELIANCE -3.3%.
3. **Among the 49, three stocks are touched: ITC (2025-01-06, +6.3%), HINDUNILVR (2025-12-05, -5.0%), RELIANCE (2023-07-20, -3.3%).** All are small steps (the series is partly adjusted, see section iii). The 49 have 32 flagged rows (>=15% close-to-close or ratio signature) in 18 stocks, but all the large ones are genuine market moves (Adani/Hindenburg Jan-Feb 2023, election day 2024-06-04, INDUSINDBK 2025-03-11, etc.).
4. **Among the 306: 13 stocks, 14 mechanical events** = 4.2% of stocks. They contaminate **3,006 stock-days through a 252-day feature window (0.80% of 378,034 bars)**, 210 label rows through a 15-day forward window (0.06%). For the 49 alone: 3 events, 691 feature-252 rows (1.12% of 61,838), 45 label-15 rows (0.07%).
5. **419 flagged rows in 183 stocks** (306 with |close-to-close| >= 15%, 85 with >= 20%, plus 113 ratio-signature-only rows below 15%). Status counts: 10 VERIFIED, 4 PARTIALLY, 50 GENUINE MOVE (19 of those are >=20%), **355 UNVERIFIED**. The UNVERIFIED rows are overwhelmingly +/-20% days with volume 5x-100x normal (NSE 20% circuit band days, INFERRED market moves). I did not search each; that is a known limit of this audit (section vi).
6. **Biggest single issue for the research conclusions:** ABFRL, SANOFI, VEDL are inside the 15D lab's 177-stock universe and were already excluded by the signed gap classification (training days removed). ALLCARGO (x2), TRIVENI, NMDC, SIEMENS, SCI, TMPV, BAJAJELEC (BAJAJELEC is listed as "unresolved" in that file but is now PARTIALLY verified as a demerger record date) are NOT covered by that file (INFERRED: because they are outside the 177 or under its >20% rule only for those 177). If any of those are in a signed training set, they are uncorrected.
7. **Gap #3 (adjusted prices) was deferred on 2026-10-05 because `split_check` found 0 suspicious moves, but that check only looked at 1 recorded split/bonus event** (`docs/brain/evidence/split_check_20261005.md`: "Split/bonus events checked: 1"), and `upcoming_events` holds only a rolling ~30-day window (13 rows, 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-23). It cannot see demergers. That conclusion ("B0 clean") is **not supported** for demergers.

## (iii) Adjusted or unadjusted? Conclusion and evidence

**Conclusion: the candles are an inconsistently adjusted series: adjusted for splits and bonuses, partially/late adjusted (or not at all) for demergers, never adjusted for dividends. It is not raw and it is not fully adjusted. (INFERRED from our data + VERIFIED against Zerodha forum statements.)**

Evidence from our data (INFERRED, reproducible with `build.py`):
- Split/bonus: no step on 5 of 5 checked ex-dates (above). Zero split signatures in 306 stocks over 5 years.
- Demergers behave in three ways:
  - (a) *Not adjusted at all*: SANOFI 2024-06-13 (our previous close 9,041.20 vs the exchange-adjusted previous close about 6,203 implied by the upper-circuit close 6,513.05 per Business Standard), TRIVENI 2026-07-22 (our previous close 471.50, news says the stock traded about 477 before and the drop was mechanical), ALLCARGO (-60%).
  - (b) *Adjusted by a factor that differs from the market's*: our pre-ex bars are scaled down but a residual step remains. ITC: our 2025-01-03 close 397.20 vs about 483 per Business Standard (ratio about 0.82), then +6.3% on the ex-date. SIEMENS: our previous close 3,754.25 vs 4,928.15 reported (ratio 0.76), then -25% residual. TMPV: our 2025-10-13 close 454.95 vs about 670 intraday reported (about 0.68), then -13% residual. ABFRL: our 203.55 vs 269.15 reported (0.76), then -56% residual. VEDL: our 404.90 vs 773.60 reported (0.52), then -33% residual. These price comparisons use news prices, not exchange bhavcopy, so the factors are approximate (INFERRED).
  - (c) *Seam artefacts with low volume and an upward step*: BAJAJELEC +38.8% (volume 0.18x), SCI +30.0% (0.47x), NMDC +85.7%. Record dates are VERIFIED, cause of the direction is UNKNOWN. For NMDC the price on the ex-date was reported at about 94-107 while our series shows 15.75 -> 29.25, implying the pre-ex and post-ex bars carry different scale factors (about 6x vs about 3.7x). INFERRED.
- Therefore the adjustment is applied by the data source at different times for different events (see Zerodha statement below), and **our stored history is a snapshot of whatever the source had adjusted on the day each stock was fetched**.

What Kite says (secondary: developer forum replies, not formal API docs; VERIFIED that these statements exist):
- Dec 2019, staff: "Adjustment is done only for splits, not other corporate actions"; adjustment is done mostly over the weekend after the corporate action; day candles adjusted, intraday only for recent actions. https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/24142/
- July 2025, staff (salim_chisty): "Adjustments are made for bonuses, splits, rights issues, spin-offs, and extraordinary dividends." On demergers: "We adjust as soon as we receive the Cost of Acquisition (COA) for the particular script." https://www.kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/51443/
- Other forum replies say split and bonus only, not dividends, and that older than about 5 years may not be adjusted; a 2025 thread reports a demerger left unadjusted. Search overview: https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/50251/ , https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/comment/24088
- Zerodha's own demerger support page only talks about adjusting the user's average buy price once the COA ratio is announced, not chart data. https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/corporate-actions/ca-others/articles/tata-motors-demerger
- So Zerodha's documented behaviour is itself inconsistent across years. Our data matches the later statement (COA-based demerger adjustment, applied late) and shows the COA factor does not equal the market's price discovery, leaving residual steps. The "no adjustment for dividends" part cannot be tested from price alone and is UNKNOWN for us.

How our ingestion stores candles (VERIFIED, code read):
- `services/ingestion.py::backfill_instrument` fetches `kite.historical_data(continuous=False, oi=False)` and **upserts** (`ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`) rows. Normal runs are **incremental**: `from_date = last stored bar - 1 day`, so history already stored is **not refetched**. A full backfill (`HISTORICAL_BACKFILL_DAYS = 1825`) rewrites everything it covers.
- Changed bars are logged to `candle_corrections` (gap #13), and first arrival to `candle_arrivals`. Local state: `candle_corrections` = 0 rows, `candle_arrivals` = 0 rows (the local DB was loaded without them), so **nothing in the local data tells us when each bar was fetched or whether it has been rewritten** (UNKNOWN).
- Consequence (INFERRED): after any new split/bonus ex-date, an incremental top-up stores the new post-split bars at the new scale next to old pre-split bars at the old scale, **creating a raw split step in our DB**, unless the stock is fully refetched. All the clean splits above happen to be in history that was loaded in one backfill after those events. `upcoming_events` already lists TAALTECH (2:10 -> 2), BLSE, BUILDPRO, BANSALWIRE, JSWDULUX, AZADIND splits and MOLDTECH/MOLDTKPAC 1:1 bonus for 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-23; none of those bars are in the local candles (data ends 2026-09-08). Prod ingests daily, so prod can already hold such steps for any stock that splits after its last full load. Not checked (prod not called).
- Nothing in `backend/src` rewrites history for corporate actions (`grep` for adjust/ex_date found only `services/split_check.py`, `m01_quality` excusal via `action_days`, `avoid.py` blackout, `what_would_change`, `tax_export`). `m01_quality` excuses big moves on recorded split/bonus days (`SPIKE_MOVE`, `action_days`), it does not adjust.

## (v) Policy recommendation (not implemented)

1. **Raw stays immutable.** `candles` untouched (CLAUDE.md principle 8). New tables next to it, read only by the brain/research:
   - `corporate_actions` (symbol/instrument_id, ex_date, action_type, ratio_num, ratio_den, price_factor, source_url, source_retrieved_at, verified_by, status VERIFIED/PARTIALLY/UNVERIFIED, notes).
   - `adjusted_candles` (or a view) = raw x factor, volume / factor, computed from `corporate_actions` rows with status VERIFIED only.
   - `adjustment_sets` (version id, sha256 of the sorted verified rows, created_at). Every model, test, replay and evidence file records the set id it used.
2. **What to adjust (only events with an external record):**
   - Split, bonus: price x 1/(ratio), volume x ratio. Standard. Since Kite already does this for these, the layer's job is to **check** (compare to the source) and to **catch un-adjusted new events** after incremental top-ups.
   - Demerger / spin-off: adjust by the *exchange-published* price reference on the ex-date (the special pre-open session price vs previous close, or the COA ratio), recorded as a separate factor with source. Do not let a source's own COA-derived scale remain mixed. Without an exchange reference, mark UNVERIFIED and **exclude the window** rather than guess.
   - Rights issue: adjust with the theoretical ex-rights price factor only when the exchange publishes it. Otherwise record, not adjust (effect is small).
   - Merger / amalgamation: the surviving symbol is continuous; the absorbed symbol ends. Record and exclude the absorbed stock after the date.
   - Buyback: no price adjustment (not a price event).
   - Dividends: ordinary dividends are NOT adjusted for a swing system (a 1-3% drop is real cash leaving; adjusting would also change every realised return). Extraordinary/special dividend that is a large share of price: record, optionally adjust, decide per case.
   - Genuine market moves (results, news, circuit hits, Hindenburg, election day, RBI actions): never adjusted.
3. **Unverifiable events:** do not guess a ratio from price. Treat a large step with no external record as `UNVERIFIED`. For training/tests: **exclude the affected stock-days** (feature rows for the window and label rows for the h bars before) in the main run and report an "include" sensitivity run, as the sl_v1 file already does for "unresolved". Genuine moves are always kept.
4. **Labels and paths:** compute 5/10/15-day labels, barriers and target/stop paths on the adjusted series (so a demerger day is a 0% step, not a stop), but keep fills and costs on the raw price that would actually trade. Where a window crosses an UNVERIFIED mechanical-looking step, drop that row from both the training and scoring sets.
5. **Detector (cheap and safe, no scraping):** nightly SELECT over our own candles for any |overnight| >= 7% with volume < 3x median, or ratio signatures, and for each hit a *manual, single-announcement lookup* (respecting `2026-10-29-nse-feed-licensing-inventory.md`: N5 corporate actions are NO-without-consent for automated use, so do NOT build the verified table by calling the NSE API; fill it by hand from public announcements, one per event; Kite instrument-list changes, ticker renames like TATAMOTORS -> TMPV, are an extra trigger).
6. **Interaction with signed results:** never overwrite. Recompute separately as new versions (e.g. `wf_v1+adj1`, `fd_v1+adj1`), side by side with the originals, labelled with the adjustment-set hash. Report the delta per result. If a delta flips a verdict, the owner decides what that means for the protocols.
7. **Prod vs local:** local candles end 2026-09-08 and have no arrivals/corrections; prod ingests daily and incrementally, so verify prod separately with a read-only API query (not done here). Add a guard: when a stock has a verified split/bonus ex-date, force a full refetch of that stock or compare overlapping bars (the correction log exists: `candle_corrections` would catch a rewrite).

**Effort (INFERRED, one developer):** schema + migration + view: 0.5 day. Hand-curated action list for the ~14 found events plus the 49's last 5 years: 1-1.5 days (mostly lookups). Detector script: 0.5 day. Wiring into brain reader and research scripts with set-hash: 1-2 days. Recompute and compare the signed results as new versions: 1-2 days. Tests: 1 day. Total about 5-8 days. Reading the licensing file, any automated source for corporate actions would need written consent or a licence (NSE corporate-data products cost INR 5,00,000 per year, per R0).

**Risks:** (a) hand-curated data goes stale, so detector plus manual review is the real control; (b) mixed scale between source adjustments and ours: Kite may later change its own adjustments (late COA), silently shifting old bars again, so snapshot adjustment sets and compare raw hashes per stock; (c) choosing demerger factors is a judgement, so label VERIFIED only with an exchange reference; (d) adjusted prices do not match what actually traded, so costs and fills must stay on raw; (e) rerunning signed results can change verdicts, which is an owner decision, not something to fold in silently.
