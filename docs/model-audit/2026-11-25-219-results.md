## Plain-English answer

None of the three candidates is a usable 5-day large-cap model on this evidence.

* **No candidate made money after costs.** ECONOMIC is **FAIL** for C1, C2 and C3 in both windows. Picks lost about 0.5% to 0.7% per trade on average after ~44 bps costs.
* **No candidate beat the average stock out-of-sample.** SELECTION is **INCONCLUSIVE** for all three in the primary window: every range includes zero.
* **The two new models are not better than model 2** in the primary window. In the longer window they are clearly worse than model 2, but model 2 is in-sample there, so that comparison is not a fair one.
* **Momentum five did no better.** It also lost money (FAIL). It was not separable from any candidate.
* The trade rule itself loses money on this list. The average scored stock lost about −0.45% per trade under the +2% / −4% / 5-session rule after costs, in almost every run.

This repeats the fd-protocol-v1 finding (FAILED 2026-10-26), this time with the signed exit rule, purged walk-forward and a pre-registered config.

## Limitations

1. **Selection bias.** The 52 are today's large caps. Point-in-time index membership was not available, so delisted or demoted stocks are missing. TMCV never became eligible (short history). This list favours survivors, so real results would likely be no better.
2. **One local snapshot,** newest session 2026-09-07. Candles are unadjusted, and the local `corporate_actions` table is empty. No >20% gap appeared in any trade window, but smaller adjustments cannot be ruled out.
3. **Model 2 held-out, not blind.** Its 20% test split (inside W_M2) produced metrics that were viewed in August 2026, so W_M2 is not a pristine forward test for C1.
4. **Three runs only in the primary window.** The block ranges are wide (about ±0.3% per trade). A small real edge could be hidden. A large one is unlikely.
5. **Five sector-unmapped symbols** (ASIANPAINT, BHARTIARTL, LTM, TMCV, TMPV) use the builder's fallback sector series. Breadth uses today's watchlist.
6. **Same trade structure as fd_v1.** +2% / −4% needs a hit rate of about 73% to break even after costs. The baseline stock loses under this rule in nearly every run, so ECONOMIC is hard to pass for any ranking.
7. **Not done:** a training-basis (old `obv_slope`) diagnostic for C1, mid/small caps (#221/#222), per-pattern models (#220), any change to model 2.
