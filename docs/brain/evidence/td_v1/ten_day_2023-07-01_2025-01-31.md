# td-protocol-v1: 10-day model, history gate

**HISTORICAL GATE - SURVIVOR-BIASED UNIVERSE (today's 49 stocks)**

**This gate only decides whether a shadow lane may be proposed. It is never proof.**
**Ranking and trading are different questions.** The first part asks whether the model's best 5 beat every stock on the same days; the second asks whether the actual trade (+6% / -3.5%, out by day 10, after costs) made money.

Rule: reach +6% before -3.5% within 10 trading days. Costs: slippage 0.10% per side in total; fills 0.05% worse.
Stocks: 49 (today's watchlist: survivor bias). Folds: 21.

- Picks beat every stock: **fail** (8 of 21 eligible folds; lower bound -0.31%).
- Trades made money after costs: **fail** (average per trade -0.34%; 1980 finished ideas).
- Every stock, same days: average per trade -0.22%.
- Reading: No evidence of skill and no profit.


Beside the model (evidence-defs-v2):
- Random 5 a day (1000 draws, mean per trade): -0.22%
- NIFTY 50 over the same holds: -0.18%
- Cash: +0.00%
- Account replay (Rs 10 lakh, ladder limits, 15% halt): best 5 -8.93% (worst fall +16.02%); 5 strongest risers -12.96%; random-5 accounts median -2.76%; NIFTY 50 held +16.33%; cash 0.00%.

Limits of this test:
- Survivor bias: the 49 stocks are today's watchlist, so stocks that were dropped or delisted are missing from the history.
- No liquidity or limit checks: fills assume the next open is executable at the stated slippage; circuit limits, halts and volume caps are not modelled. To be resolved before any realistic portfolio simulation or trading approval.
- Costs use the shared scorer's Rs 40,000 position; the protocol's Rs 1,00,000 applies to the forward lane.
- Stop and target levels follow the slippage-adjusted fill, as the shared scorer does.
- The 10-day history is partly already seen (protocol section 3): a pass allows a watch-only lane, never proof.
- Drawdown and account returns come from a simulated Rs 10 lakh account and are reported only; benchmarks are NIFTY 50, every stock, random picks and cash.
