# 5-day strategy, layer 1 (fd-protocol-v1)

Every number on this page is a **5-day** figure (the 5-day v1 strategy). None is a 15-day figure; never add or compare them with the 15-day brain's numbers without the label.

Protocol: fd-protocol-v1 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-five-day-protocol.md`). Sample: 2023-07-01 to 2025-01-31, confirmation (pass or fail, layer 1). 49 stocks.
5-day model question: endpoint: close 5 trading days later at least +2% above the decision day's close (as prod models 2, 16, 17). 5-day trade scored: next open; +2% target / -4% stop from the decision day's close; trailing stop; no half-out; sold at the next open after a close scoring at or below 0.35; time stop at the open of the first trading day on or after entry + 30 calendar days; costs and slippage. Target and stop on the same day count as the stop.

## Limits

- Sample (section 5): 'never scored with a 5-day rule' is not 'never seen'. The confirmation period 2023-07-01 to 2025-01-31 was studied for the 15-day brain (walk-forward, bad-market windows, cost stress).
- Survivor bias: the universe is today's 49 model stocks; stocks delisted or dropped earlier are missing.
- Daily candles cannot say whether target or stop came first on the same day: counted as the stop.
- Layer 1 has no account: every buy-rule idea is scored on its own (no slot limit, no 'already held', no risk sizing). The account replay below is reported only.
- Daily candles only: prod checks exits every minute; here a raised trailing stop counts on the day it rises only when the close is at or below it, and exits at an open use the open price.
- Prod scores with 3 cap-tier models (main, midcap, smallcap); this tool trains one endpoint model per fold on the 49 stocks, like the 15-day protocol. It is not the exact model in prod.
- A trade decided near a fold's end is watched for the confidence exit by the next fold's model (the last fold's model after the sample ends), as prod watches with whichever model is active.
- The v1 bot and the brain share the paper account's slots, so forward results are not independent until D6.
- A pass never justifies live trading. Layer 2, manual approval and the owner's go-live call come first.

Layer 2: not built: the forward test waits for the owner's D6 / tv22 decision.
