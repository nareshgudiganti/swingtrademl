# First as-of replay: 13 July to 14 August 2026

Run 2026-10-11/12 on the local database with `swingtrade brain replay-score --from 2026-07-13 --to 2026-08-14
--asof-model swing_classifier_asof_20260710`. Raw data: `asof_replay_2026-07-13_2026-08-14.json`.

## Plain summary

The model was trained only on rows whose outcome was known by 10 July (45,597 rows, held-out ROC AUC 0.568).
It then scored each of the next 25 trading days. Every idea had its full 15 trading days to play out.

| | Ideas | Reached target first | Average result per idea, after costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brain (model score 60% or more) | 561 | 19% (106) | −1.0% |
| Random picks, same number each day, 200 tries | | | −1.0% (middle 90%: −1.3% to −0.8%) |
| Every stock the brain looked at (7,600) | | 22% | −0.4% |
| Version 1's buys on the same days | 20 | 25% | −0.3% |

About 33% must reach the target to break even before costs.

**Reading:** on these 25 days the brain's picks were no better than random picks on the same days. They were also
worse than taking every stock. Higher scores did a little better (80+: 26% of 19 ideas, 70-79: 22%, 60-69: 17%),
which is the right direction but far too few ideas in the top band to trust.

## What is inside the number

- **Two different periods.** Before 5 August: 95 ideas over 17 days, 35% reached the target, average **+0.6%**.
  From 5 August: 466 ideas over 8 days, 16% reached the target, average **−1.3%**. From 5 August the model scored
  44 to 79 stocks a day above 60%, a market-wide dip. 83% of all ideas come from those 8 days.
- **So the real sample is small.** Counting each day once, the average is −0.07% and 12 of 25 days were positive.
  561 ideas from one 8-day episode is not 561 independent pieces of evidence.
- **The model behaves like a dip buyer.** It flooded ideas on a falling market, and the falling market kept falling.
  The full brain would have been DEFENSIVE then and may have taken few or none; this replay does not show that.

## What this does and does not test

- It tests the model score at the 60% buy level, scored without any look-ahead (causality and no-look-ahead tests pass).
- It does **not** test the whole brain. M05 memory, M06 combiner and M12 stock brain are in shadow and not part of
  this. The risk gate in a replay reads today's portfolio, so all 561 ideas were "blocked by the risk check" and the
  portfolio track is empty. A point-in-time portfolio simulator is needed for that.
- One month, one model, today's watchlist (survivor bias before 5 Oct), daily candles only (a day touching both
  levels is counted as the stop; 28 exits filled at a gap).

## Consequence for the strength gates

Gate 2 (beats chance) and gate 3 (beats alternatives) are **not met** on this evidence. Gate 5 (different markets)
shows the model is regime dependent. This is consistent with M06 sitting below break-even. The next useful
experiments: replay 6 to 12 months with a model per cutoff, and a replay that includes the market-mode gate and a
point-in-time portfolio.
