    Six principles, in priority order. When two conflict, the lower number wins.

    #PrincipleWhat it means in practice
    1Safety rules never change ✎Stop, risk per trade, position caps, sector cap and the drawdown halt are locked. No plug-in, plan, setting or learning step can change them.
    2No uncontrolled changesModels, rules and playbooks change only through tests, a shadow run and your approval. Nothing changes itself live.
    3No silent failuresDetect → report → recover → learn. Missing or stale data lowers quality and can stop a decision; it never passes silently.
    4Everything is a plug-in, behind a stable contractNew data, models, strategies and outputs plug in through versioned contracts without touching the core.
    5Evidence before claims ✎Every probability, score and return shows its track record and sample size. Nothing is shown as a promise.
    6Replaceable where it pays ✎Broker, cloud and screens can be swapped through adapters. The database changes only through a planned migration.

    Read top to bottom: data comes in, is checked and put into common formats, the event bus runs the brain, every decision passes the safety layer, and each plan sees its share of the outputs. The foundation underneath keeps it learning, observable and replaceable.
